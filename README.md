
# Hi, I'm Conor Smalley 👋

**Developer | Home Automation Enthusiast | Self-Hosting Advocate | Martial Arts Instructor**

I'm a UK-based developer who enjoys building practical software, automating everyday tasks, and experimenting with technology.

I enjoy working across different languages and technologies, particularly building applications, integrating APIs, and developing solutions to problems I've encountered in everyday life.

I'm also a big fan of self-hosting, home automation, and getting the most out of the hardware and software I already have.

---

## 🚀 What I'm Working On

A few areas I'm particularly interested in:

- **Home Automation** – Integrating services, building custom integrations and automating everyday household tasks.
- **Self-Hosting** – Running and maintaining my own services, experimenting with Docker, networking and infrastructure.
- **Application Development** – Building web applications, APIs and tools that solve practical problems.
- **API Integrations** – Connecting different platforms and services to make them work together.
- **Open Source** – Exploring existing projects, contributing where I can, and building tools that others might find useful.

---

## 🔧 Projects

Some of the areas you'll find across my repositories include:
